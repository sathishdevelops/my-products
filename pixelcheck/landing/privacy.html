Privacy

What PixelCheck reads, and what it does not.

PixelCheck is a Chrome extension that audits advertising and analytics tags on the tab you have open, then can export a PDF on your computer.

Data we access

When you click the PixelCheck icon, the extension reads the current page’s URL, script tags, and in-page tag objects (for example GTM, GA4, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Consent Mode, and UTM parameters). That happens only for the active tab, and only after you click.

A license key you paste is stored locally in Chrome storage so the PDF watermark can be removed. The key may contain the email used when the key was issued. It is verified on your device.

Data we do not collect

PixelCheck does not send page contents, URLs, or license keys to our servers. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no ad network inside the extension. We do not sell or share user data. We do not inject ads or affiliate links into websites.

Optional purchase

Scanning is free. Unwatermarked PDFs are an optional paid license sold on a checkout page you open yourself (for example Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy). Payment is handled by that provider, not by PixelCheck’s servers.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the email on your Chrome Web Store listing.
