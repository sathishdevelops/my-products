Privacy

What BugBrief reads, and what it does not.

BugBrief is a free Chrome extension that scans the tab you have open for QA issues, can capture screenshots, copy a bug report on your computer, and can generate test data.

Data we access

A content script records console errors and unhandled promise rejections on http(s) pages so they can appear in a scan. When you click the BugBrief icon, the extension also reads the current page’s URL, title, viewport, user-agent, DOM (images, forms, headings, links), resource timing, local/session storage counts, and optional screenshots of the visible tab or a stitched full page. Tools can show password fields on the page, outline controls, disable stylesheets, clear this origin’s localStorage/sessionStorage, and resize the current Chrome window. That data stays on your device.

Data we do not collect

BugBrief does not send page contents, URLs, or screenshots to our servers. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no ad network inside the extension. We do not sell or share user data. We do not inject ads or affiliate links into websites.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the email on your Chrome Web Store listing.
